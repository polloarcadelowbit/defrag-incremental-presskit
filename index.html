ABOUT

A defrag utility that got a bit out of hand

Defrag Incremental is an incremental game about taking disk jobs and trying to get through a workday with an increasingly upgraded old PC.

At first it is very simple: pick a disk, choose how to process it and finish the job. Later, disks get bigger, hotter, damaged and more valuable. You have to think more about the algorithm, the mode, the condition of the disk and how far you want to push the machine.

Temporary upgrades improve the current run. Rebooting resets that run progress, converts Reboot Charge into BIOS Chips and lets you buy permanent BIOS upgrades that stay between runs.

Automation appears later and handles some of the routine work, but it is deliberately not perfect. Playing actively is normally faster and safer.

VERSION 1.1

The interesting stuff starts earlier now

Version 1.1 was released on 4 September 2026. The main goal was pretty simple: make the interesting parts of the game happen earlier.

Heat and disk health now matter much sooner. Defragging can trigger active disk events that need a decision. The economy and life layer was rebuilt, upgrade information is clearer, permanent BIOS purchases are easier to inspect, and more of the game reacts to what the player is doing during a normal workday.

The update also improves window resizing and aspect-ratio support, adds manual saving, clearer system-stat panels, and fixes several progression and UI problems reported after launch.
